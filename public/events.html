Speaking & Community Impact

Bryce Perry speaks at Parkinson's conferences, community gatherings, and advocacy events across North America. His talks combine lived experience, practical strategies, and honest conversations about the realities of living with Parkinson's.

Through the Doing Life Today platform - including podcasts, YouTube programs, livestreams, and community conversations - Bryce now connects with thousands of people navigating Parkinson's around the world.

When Bryce speaks at an event, we often organize a Doing Life Today community meetup so attendees can connect in person, share their stories, and meet others living with Parkinson's.

Book Bryce to Speak

Recent speaking engagements include Parkinson Canada · Michael J. Fox Foundation events · OPFNE Parkinson's Symposium · Living Better with Parkinson's Conference · LSU Louisiana Parkinson's Conference · World Parkinson Congress community gatherings.
