What TrendSense Is

An intelligence layer built from lived experience.

TrendSense transforms anonymized community behavior, self-tracking data, and AI coach intent signals from the Doing Life Today platform into actionable insights for research, care innovation, clinical trial planning, patient support, and product partnership.

Behavior

Daily check-ins, engagement patterns, and community signals from people living with Parkinson's worldwide.

Medication

Real-world medication logging, adherence patterns, and on-time signals across good days and rough days.

Symptoms

Symptom burden, off-state patterns, sleep, mood, freezing, falls, and care partner observations.

AI Intent

What members ask their AI coach - topics, concerns, and emerging conversation themes that surface unmet needs.
