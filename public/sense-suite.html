New in The Club · Sense Suite

Know your personal Parkinson's baseline.

Four short check-ins on your phone — voice, movement, fine motor and thinking. Sense Suite builds your baseline, then shows how each measure changes over time. No population averages. Just you, compared with you.

Start free in The Club See plans

Four ways to measure yourself

Each check-in takes a few minutes on the phone or tablet you already have. Do three to build your baseline, then come back to see what's changing.

VoiceSense

Voice

Voice strength, sustained voice, pitch and pace. Recordings are processed on your device and never uploaded.

MoveSense

Movement

Chair stands, walking pace, step consistency and turning, using your phone's motion sensor.

MotorSense

Fine motor

Tapping speed and rhythm, alternating taps, target accuracy and tracing steadiness.

MindSense

Thinking

Attention, response speed, working memory, mental flexibility and sequence recall.

Bring it to your doctor. Talk it through with Lil Bryce.

Doctor Visit Report

With Sense Suite+, your results are added to your Doctor Visit Report — baseline, latest reading and what changed, ready to print or send to your care team.

Lil Bryce

Lil Bryce can see your results and talk them through with you — calmly, without alarm, and always pointing back to your care team for medical questions.

Saved to your account

Your measurements are saved privately to your Club account, so they follow you to a new phone.

Questions

Is Sense Suite free?

Yes. Every member of The Club can build a free baseline in all four tools and do one retest every 30 days. Sense Suite+ adds unlimited check-ins, guided practice and your results in your Doctor Visit Report.

Does Sense Suite diagnose Parkinson's?

No. Sense Suite is for personal tracking. It compares you with your own baseline, not with other people, and it is not a medical test. Talk with your care team about any change that worries you.

What do I need?

A phone or tablet. The movement and tapping checks need a touchscreen and a phone you can hold or keep at your waist. Sign in to The Club so your results are saved to your account.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Plans are billed through Stripe and you can cancel anytime from Billing in The Club. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.

I'm in the Inner Circle. What do I pay?

TrendSense at half price — monthly if your Inner Circle is monthly, yearly if your Inner Circle is yearly. It's applied automatically when you check out signed in to The Club.

Sense Suite measurements are patient-generated and for personal tracking only. Sense Suite is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or monitor any condition. Results can be affected by phone placement, time since medication, sleep and other factors.
