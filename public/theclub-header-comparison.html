What this comparison shows

  • Option A - Even at full resolution, the lighthouse collapses to noise at 130px tall. The header reads as a vague band of dark blue. White brand text and tab labels need extra shadows to stay legible. Every screen pays the photo's bandwidth cost.
  • Option B - The icon mark on charcoal gives instant brand recognition without competing with content. Tabs stay sharp, navigation feels light, the page scrolls fast. This is what users will look at hundreds of times a day.
  • Option C - The photo lands hard when it gets the whole stage. Welcome screen, login, onboarding, milestone celebrations, empty states, share cards - that's where it makes people feel something. Reserve it for those moments and it stays powerful.
