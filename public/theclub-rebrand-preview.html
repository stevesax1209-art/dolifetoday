Note on the mark itself

The current lockup needs a light variant

The supplied lockup is built on charcoal - silver and gold against deep navy. To use it on cream/white surfaces above, we'd recolor the silver pillar to charcoal and the bright teal to deep teal #0E7A77.

Recommendation: Export a light-background variant of the stacked + horizontal lockups before launch - for print, email signatures, partner co-branding, and any non-dark surface.
