Parkinson's Podcast for Newly Diagnosed: Where to Start

When you are newly diagnosed, the right podcast should make Parkinson's feel more understandable, not more overwhelming. That is exactly where Living with Parkinson's can help.

The first problem after a Parkinson's diagnosis is not just information overload. It is trust overload. There is too much to read, too many opinions, and not enough guidance that feels like a real human being walking you through it.

That is why a good Parkinson's podcast for newly diagnosed listeners needs three things:

  • Clear language instead of jargon
  • Topics that match the questions you are already asking
  • A voice that understands the emotional side of diagnosis too

Living with Parkinson's works well here because Bryce Perry speaks directly to those real-life questions. Episodes regularly cover medication, symptoms, voice changes, care partners, and decisions people face in the early stages.

If you want a next step beyond the podcast, pair it with the First 90 Days guide and the broader Best Parkinson's Podcasts page.

Best place to begin

Start on the Living with Parkinson's show hub. You can hear the newest episode first, then search the archive for topics that match what you are dealing with right now.
