From Bryce

Why This Recognition Matters

This is more than a trophy.

When Bryce Perry started Doing Life Today, the goal was not to win awards. The goal was to help people with Parkinson's feel less alone.

The podcast was built around real conversations. Medication timing. Fatigue. Stiffness. Anxiety. Side effects. Care partner struggles. The quiet fears people carry. The questions people search for at midnight when they do not know who else to ask.

To see that work recognized with the Award of Excellence in Healthcare & Pharma means Parkinson's stories are being seen at the highest level of communications.

This is a win for Bryce. This is a win for Doing Life Today. But more than anything, this is a win for the Parkinson's community.

A note from Bryce Perry

When I started Doing Life Today, I wasn't trying to win awards. I was trying to help people with Parkinson's feel less alone. To have a Parkinson's podcast recognized with the Award of Excellence in Healthcare & Pharma is incredibly meaningful. This is a win for the whole Parkinson's community.

Official Announcement

Doing Life Today Wins Top Healthcare & Pharma Podcast Series Honor at the 2026 Communicator Awards

For Immediate Release · May 5, 2026

Doing Life Today recognized with three 2026 Communicator Awards, including the Award of Excellence in Healthcare & Pharma Podcast Series.

Doing Life Today, the Parkinson's media and community platform founded by Bryce Perry, has been recognized with three wins at the 32nd Annual Communicator Awards, including the 2026 Communicator Award of Excellence for Series — Healthcare & Pharma for Podcasts for Living with Parkinson's: The Good, the Bad, and the Reality.

The podcast also received two 2026 Communicator Awards of Distinction: one for Individual Episodes — Healthcare & Pharma for Podcasts for How Much Levodopa Is Too Much? The Signs Nobody Warned Me About, and one for Series — Video Podcasts (Healthcare & Pharma) for Podcasts for Living with Parkinson's: The Good, the Bad, and the Reality.

Bryce Perry is a 2026 Communicator Awards winner and an award-winning Parkinson's podcast host and creator.

The Honor

About the Communicator Awards

The Communicator Awards recognize excellence, effectiveness, and innovation across communication, marketing, media, and creative work. The 32nd Annual Communicator Awards received thousands of entries from agencies, companies, nonprofits, creators, and communications professionals around the world.

The Award of Excellence is the highest honor given by the Communicator Awards.

Doing Life Today was recognized alongside winners from major organizations, agencies, creators, and global brands across many communication categories.

The Podcast

About the Award-Winning Parkinson's Podcast

Living with Parkinson's: The Good, the Bad, and the Reality is an award-winning podcast hosted by Bryce Perry, a person living with Parkinson's since age 40.

The show explores the real-life side of Parkinson's: the symptoms people do not always talk about, the questions people are afraid to ask, and the emotional reality of living with a progressive condition while still trying to live fully.

The podcast is part of Doing Life Today, a growing Parkinson's media and community platform reaching people in more than 120 countries through videos, podcasts, tools, music, and community support.
