Clinical Trial Match

Find Parkinson's Clinical Trials That Actually Fit You

There are thousands of Parkinson's studies on ClinicalTrials.gov. Most of them have nothing to do with you.

Clinical Trial Match is the Parkinson's trial finder built into The Club. It pulls live studies from ClinicalTrials.gov and ranks them against your diagnosis, location, age, and what you actually want to participate in - with one-click tools for the conversation with your doctor.

Our Story

Why Doing Life Today Exists

Bryce Perry was diagnosed with Parkinson's disease at age 40.

Like many people facing a Parkinson's diagnosis, the early years were filled with confusion, unanswered questions, and uncertainty about what life would look like moving forward.

What began as a personal journey quickly turned into a mission to help others navigate the disease with clarity, support, and honest information.

Through podcasts, videos, and a growing global community, Bryce now connects people around the world with practical insight and real conversations about living with Parkinson's.

The mission is simple: empower people living with Parkinson's to take back their lives and build a community where no one faces Parkinson's alone.

Global Reach

A Global Parkinson's Community

Doing Life Today connects people navigating Parkinson's across the world.

Through podcasts, videos, and community conversations, the platform now reaches listeners and viewers in more than 120 countries.

What started as one person sharing his experience has grown into a global network of people supporting each other through the realities of Parkinson's.

No matter where someone lives, the goal remains the same: helping people feel informed, supported, and less alone.

You Don't Have to Do This Alone

The Club is the private community created through Doing Life Today where people living with Parkinson's and care partners connect, share experiences, and support one another.

Inside the community members find conversations, encouragement, and practical insight from others navigating the same journey.
