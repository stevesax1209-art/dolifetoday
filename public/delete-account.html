Request Account Deletion

We respect your privacy and provide a clear way to request deletion of your account and personal data.

How to Delete Your Account

Option 1 - In the app (fastest):

Open The Club app → tap your profile → go to Settings → Delete Account.

Option 2 - Email us:

Send a deletion request to:

📧 support@dolifetoday.com

Please include:

  • The email address associated with your account
  • Subject line: "Account Deletion Request"
Request Deletion via Email

What Happens Next

  • We will process your request within 7 days
  • We may contact you to verify your identity for security purposes
  • You will receive a confirmation once your account has been deleted

What Data Is Deleted

Upon account deletion, the following data will be permanently removed:

  • Account information (name, email address)
  • Profile data (demographics, preferences, settings)
  • User-generated content (posts, comments, and messages where applicable)
  • Health and lifestyle tracking data (symptoms, medications, check-ins)

Data Retention

In certain cases, limited data may be retained temporarily for:

  • Compliance with legal obligations
  • Resolution of disputes or enforcement of agreements
  • Fraud prevention and security purposes

Any retained data is securely handled and will be deleted once the retention period has expired.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about account deletion or data privacy, please contact us at:

📧 office@dolifetoday.com
