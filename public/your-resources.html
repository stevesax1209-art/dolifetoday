Frequently Asked Questions

What is a personalized Parkinson's plan in The Club?
It is a one-page action plan generated from your own tracker data and daily check-ins inside The Club. You pick a topic like fatigue, sleep, or anxiety, and the system builds a resource with insights, next steps, and practical guidance based on your real patterns.
How does The Club personalize my plan?
The Club pulls from your symptom logs, medication tracking, exercise data, sleep entries, and daily check-ins. The more consistently you log, the more specific and useful each plan becomes.
What topics can my personalized plan cover?
You can choose from fatigue, sleep, anxiety, rough day resets, medication routines, wearing off, exercise, meal timing, doctor visit prep, travel with Parkinson's, newly diagnosed guidance, care partner conversations, and motivation slumps.
Do I need to pay for this feature?
No. Personalized plans are a free feature available to all members of The Club. You can join at club.dolifetoday.com.
How long does it take to generate a plan?
Seconds. You pick your topic, optionally add context, and your one-pager is ready almost instantly.
Can I build more than one plan?
Yes. You can build as many plans as you want, on any topic, anytime. Your saved plans live in your Resources tab so you can revisit them.
