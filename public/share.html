Share Kit

Most people with Parkinson's never hear about communities like ours. Your share - to a friend, a support group, a clinician - can be the moment that changes someone's whole experience of this disease.

🎬 Videos

15-second commercials and short clips. Tap a video to watch, then download or share.

🖼️ Social Graphics

Posts and quote cards sized for Facebook, Instagram, and X. Right-click or long-press to save, or use the buttons below each.

📄 Printables & PDFs

One-pagers and flyers for support group leaders, neuros, PTs, and anyone you want to hand a piece of paper to.

Why this matters

Every week we hear from people who say "I wish I'd known about this years ago." Word-of-mouth from members like you is how this community grows - not ads, not algorithms. If even one person you share with finds something helpful inside, it was worth the 30 seconds.
