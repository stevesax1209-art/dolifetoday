Our Shows

Two Shows. One Podcast Network.

The Doing Life Today Podcast Network brings together conversations, stories, and perspectives from people living with Parkinson's and those walking beside them.

Each show explores different parts of the journey — the challenges, the humor, the resilience, and the everyday moments that define life with Parkinson's.

Living with Parkinson's: The Good, the Bad, and the Reality

The flagship show from Doing Life Today, hosted by Bryce Perry. Raw, honest conversations about every aspect of life with Parkinson's. From diagnosis day to decade five. Real stories, expert guests, and no topic off limits.

🎧 127+ Episodes 📅 Weekly ⏱️ 30–60 min
🎵 Spotify 🎧 Show Hub ▶ YouTube

Life in Motion: The Parkinson's Podcast

Hosted by Bryce Perry and Brian Campkin, both living with Parkinson's themselves. Life in Motion blends humor, honesty, and community connection with conversations about daily life, exercise, and navigating the condition together.

🎧 55+ Episodes 📅 Weekly · Sundays 12pm EST ⏱️ 40–55 min

Be on the Show

Want to Play Shakey or Fakey?

Think you can beat Brian?

We're inviting people living with Parkinson's to join us live on Life in Motion: The Parkinson's Podcast to play our fan-favorite game:

🌀 Shakey or Fakey

During the segment, you'll go head-to-head with Brian as Bryce reads out strange, surprising, or hilarious Parkinson's scenarios. Your job?

Guess whether the story is:

  • 🌀Shakey - a real Parkinson's experience
  • 🎭Fakey - something Bryce completely made up

No preparation needed. No pressure. Just a few minutes of fun, laughter, and real Parkinson's moments with the community.

If you love the show and think you've got a good instinct for spotting the real stories...

Come play the game with us.

Apply to Be a Contestant →

Who Can Play?

We're looking for people in the Parkinson's community who want to join the fun.

  • ✓People living with Parkinson's
  • ✓Care partners and family members
  • ✓Anyone in the community who enjoys the show
  • ✓Comfortable joining us briefly on video or audio
  • ✓Ready to have a laugh with Bryce and Brian

No experience needed. Just bring yourself and your best guess.

Brian's record is on the line... 👀
