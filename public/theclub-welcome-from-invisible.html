What The Club is

A free, private community for people living with Parkinson’s and their care partners. Built by people living with Parkinson’s. Available in 30 languages.

Inside you’ll find personal & care partner dashboards, symptom & medication trackers, a built-in AI coach (Lil Bryce), and topic circles for sleep, movement, medication, and everyday life with Parkinson’s.

No ads. No trolls. No one trying to sell you anything. The Club will always be free to join.
