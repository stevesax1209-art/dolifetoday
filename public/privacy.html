🔒 Privacy

Privacy Policy

How Doing Life Today collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

Our Commitment to Your Privacy

Doing Life Today respects your privacy. We are committed to protecting your personal information and being transparent about how it is used within our website and mobile applications.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information when you use our platform:

  • Name and email address when creating an account
  • Profile information you choose to provide
  • Content you share within the community (posts, comments, check-ins)
  • Health and lifestyle information you choose to track (such as symptoms, medications, sleep, meals, and activity)
  • Usage data to improve app performance and user experience

We only collect information that you voluntarily provide or that is necessary for the functionality of the platform.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and operate the community platform
  • Personalize your experience
  • Enable tracking tools and insights
  • Improve features and functionality
  • Communicate with you about updates or important information

We do not sell your personal information.

Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party services (such as email providers or analytics tools) to support our platform. These services only access information necessary to perform their function and are required to protect your data.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

  • Account information (name, email, profile details): retained for as long as your account is active.
  • Health and tracking data (symptoms, medications, sleep, meals, exercise logs): retained for as long as your account is active so you can access historical trends and reports.
  • Community content (posts, comments, messages): retained for as long as your account is active or as needed for community integrity.
  • Usage and analytics data: retained in anonymized or aggregated form for up to 26 months to improve the platform.

When you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain certain information for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes.

Account Deletion

You may request to delete your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at:

📧 support@dolifetoday.com

We will process all deletion requests within 30 days of receiving your request.

Your Choices

You may unsubscribe from email communications at any time using the unsubscribe link included in emails.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this policy, you can contact us at:

📧 support@dolifetoday.com
