FAQ

Questions? Answered.

Is the basic listing really free?

Yes. You can create a listing at no cost, no credit card required. Upgrade to Verified or Community whenever you are ready.

What does Verified add over Basic?

A verified badge, logo and photo uploads, direct contact buttons (website, email, phone, booking), full listing control, and views/click analytics.

What is the Community Provider channel?

Community Providers get a dedicated branded channel inside THE CLUB where they can post content, build followers, and engage with the Parkinson's community directly.

Do you support providers outside the US?

Absolutely. The Exchange serves providers in 12+ countries. Global visibility is a core feature.

Can I upgrade or downgrade anytime?

Yes. Switch tiers whenever your needs change. No long-term contracts.

How long does verification take?

Most listings are reviewed within 3 to 5 business days.
