Questions

What people ask before joining

Who is Inner Circle for?

People with Parkinson's and care partners who want closer access to Bryce, a smaller monthly conversation, and a more personal layer of support inside The Club.

Do I have to talk on the Zoom calls?

No. You can join quietly, listen, use the chat, or participate when you feel ready. There is no pressure to be on camera.

Is The Club still free?

Yes. The Club remains free. Inner Circle is an optional monthly live call and deeper-access layer for members who want it.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Monthly access is handled through Stripe and can be cancelled from your billing portal. Annual access is available for members who prefer one payment for the year.

Come closer. Bring the real day with you.

You do not need to have perfect words, a good day, or a polished question. Inner Circle is built for real life.
