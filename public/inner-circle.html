What Is the Inner Circle?

The Inner Circle is a premium experience inside The Club — Doing Life Today's private Parkinson's community. It's built for people who want more than surface-level conversation. More real talk, more direct access, more connection with others who truly get it.

Membership

What You Get

🎥

Monthly Live Zoom Calls

Join Bryce for honest, unscripted conversations about living with Parkinson's · ask questions, share your story, and hear from others in real time.

👥

A Smaller, Closer Group

Not a crowd · a circle. This is a tight-knit group of people who show up for each other, where every voice matters.

⭐

Priority Responses & Support

Get faster responses from Bryce and the team. Your questions and posts get priority attention inside The Club.

💬

Deeper Conversations

Go beyond the basics. Talk about the stuff that's hard to say out loud · fears, frustrations, wins, and everything in between.

📞

Direct Access to Bryce

Bryce Perry, diagnosed at 40, shares what he's learned firsthand. Inner Circle members get direct access to his experience and perspective.

🔒

Exclusive Inner Circle Space

A dedicated area inside The Club just for members · your space for the conversations that matter most.
