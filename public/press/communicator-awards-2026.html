Doing Life Today Wins Three 2026 Communicator Awards, Including the Award of Excellence for Healthcare & Pharma Podcast Series

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE · May 5, 2026 · Doing Life Today

Doing Life Today, the Parkinson's media and community platform founded by Bryce Perry, has been recognized with three wins at the 32nd Annual Communicator Awards, including the 2026 Communicator Award of Excellence for Series — Healthcare & Pharma for Podcasts for the show Living with Parkinson's: The Good, the Bad, and the Reality.

The Award of Excellence is the highest honor given by the Communicator Awards. The podcast also received two 2026 Communicator Awards of Distinction, recognizing both an individual episode on Parkinson's medication and the show's video podcast format.

The Three 2026 Communicator Award Wins

  • 🏆 Award of Excellence Series — Healthcare & Pharma for Podcasts
    Living with Parkinson's: The Good, the Bad, and the Reality
  • ✦ Award of Distinction Individual Episodes — Healthcare & Pharma for Podcasts
    How Much Levodopa Is Too Much? The Signs Nobody Warned Me About
  • ✦ Award of Distinction Series — Video Podcasts (Healthcare & Pharma) for Podcasts
    Living with Parkinson's: The Good, the Bad, and the Reality

About the Recognition

The Communicator Awards recognize excellence, effectiveness, and innovation across communication, marketing, media, and creative work. The 32nd Annual Communicator Awards received thousands of entries from agencies, companies, nonprofits, creators, and communications professionals around the world. Doing Life Today was recognized alongside winners from major organizations, agencies, creators, and global brands across many communication categories.

About the Podcast

Living with Parkinson's: The Good, the Bad, and the Reality is hosted by Bryce Perry, a person living with Parkinson's since age 40. The show explores the real-life side of Parkinson's: the symptoms people do not always talk about, the questions people are afraid to ask, and the emotional reality of living with a progressive condition while still trying to live fully.

Episodes cover medication timing, levodopa, fatigue, anxiety, sleep, speech changes, mental health, care partner relationships, and the everyday questions people search for at midnight when they do not know who else to ask.

From the Founder

When I started Doing Life Today, I wasn't trying to win awards. I was trying to help people with Parkinson's feel less alone. To have a Parkinson's podcast recognized with the Award of Excellence in Healthcare & Pharma is incredibly meaningful. This is a win for the whole Parkinson's community. — Bryce Perry, Founder of Doing Life Today

About Doing Life Today

Doing Life Today is a Parkinson's media and community platform founded by Bryce Perry, a person living with Parkinson's since age 40. The platform reaches people in more than 120 countries through podcasts, videos, music, daily check-in tools, symptom tracking, and a global online community known as The Club. Doing Life Today exists to help people with Parkinson's and their care partners feel less alone, more informed, and more empowered to keep doing life today.

About Bryce Perry

Bryce Perry is the founder of Doing Life Today, an award-winning Parkinson's creator, speaker, podcast host, and community builder. Diagnosed with Parkinson's at age 40, Bryce now uses his lived experience to advocate for and support the global Parkinson's community.
