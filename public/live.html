The Club

What is The Club?

The Club is a place for people living with Parkinson's, care partners, and supporters to connect, learn, track patterns, find encouragement, and keep moving forward together. It's built around real life, real support, and real community.

Community, tracking, and support all in one place.

Why I built this

I've been living with Parkinson's for over 15 years, and I know how easy it is to feel like you're figuring everything out on your own. I created Doing Life Today and The Club to help people live better now - not just wait and hope for someday.
