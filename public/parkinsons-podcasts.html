Best Parkinson's Podcasts to Listen to Right Now

Looking for the best Parkinson's podcasts? Start with real conversations, lived experience, and practical insight from Bryce Perry and the Doing Life Today podcast network.

Show #1

Living with Parkinson's: The Good, the Bad, and the Reality

Hosted by Parkinson's advocate Bryce Perry, Living with Parkinson's is the flagship Parkinson's podcast from Doing Life Today. It explores the real-life experience of living with Parkinson's through honest conversations, practical advice, and topics people actually search for when they need help.

If you are searching for a Parkinson's podcast about symptoms, medication strategies, mental health, daily routines, care partners, and the messy reality of the disease, this is the best place to start.

Show #2

Life in Motion: The Parkinson's Podcast

Life in Motion is a podcast hosted by Bryce Perry and Brian Campkin. Both hosts live with Parkinson's themselves, bringing humor, honesty, and community connection to conversations about the disease.

Episodes include discussions about daily life with Parkinson's, community stories, and insights about navigating the condition. The show blends information with humor and shared experience.

🎵 Spotify 🎧 Show Hub ▶ YouTube
Why Podcasts Help

Why Podcasts Matter for People with Parkinson's

Living with Parkinson's often raises many questions. Podcasts allow people to hear discussions about treatment options, lifestyle changes, emotional challenges, exercise and wellness, and research and medical updates.

Hearing real conversations can help people feel informed, supported, and less alone. Many of our listeners say the podcast was their first step toward finding the Doing Life Today community.

📻

Topics Covered

  • 💊Treatment options and medication
  • 🌿Lifestyle changes and daily routines
  • 😊Emotional challenges and mental health
  • 🏃Exercise and wellness strategies
  • 🔬Research updates and clinical trials
  • 🤝Caregiving and family dynamics
Where to start

Which Parkinson's podcast should you begin with?

If you want the most complete introduction to real life with Parkinson's, start with Living with Parkinson's. It is the core show in the network and the strongest entry point for listeners looking for honest, practical conversations about the disease.

If you want a second show with more community chemistry, movement topics, and humor between two hosts living with Parkinson's, move next to Life in Motion.

FAQ

Questions people ask about Parkinson's podcasts

Strong options include Living with Parkinson's for honest day-to-day conversations and Life in Motion for a mix of humor, movement, and community-driven discussion.

Living with Parkinson's is a strong starting point because it covers symptoms, medication, mindset, care partners, and the emotional reality of a diagnosis in a direct, understandable way.

About Doing Life Today

Real Talk. Real Life. Real Community.

Doing Life Today is a platform created by Parkinson's advocate Bryce Perry. After being diagnosed with Parkinson's at age 40, Bryce began sharing his experiences publicly to help others better understand the disease and navigate life with it.

Today the platform includes podcasts, videos, programs, and a growing community for people navigating Parkinson's.
