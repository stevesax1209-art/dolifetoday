A Private Space Just For Us

Not Another Public Facebook Group

Most Parkinson's groups online are public.

Anyone can join.

Anyone can comment.

Anyone can watch.

The Club is different.

It's a private community exclusively for people living with Parkinson's and their care partners.

That means:

• No random internet trolls

• No advertisers jumping into conversations

• No outsiders trying to explain Parkinson's to the people actually living with it

Just people who understand.

For people living with Parkinson's.
For care partners walking beside them.
For no one else.

Some members come on their own.
Some come with someone beside them.
Both are welcome inside the Club.

Bring Your Person

Bring Your Person (If You Have One)

Parkinson's journeys look different for everyone.

Some members navigate it independently.

Others have a partner, spouse, or loved one walking beside them.

When the Club opens, members will have the option to invite one person connected to their Parkinson's journey.

If you're living with Parkinson's, you can invite a care partner.

If you're a care partner, you can invite the person you support.

Each person receives their own membership and login inside the community.

No shared accounts.

Because everyone affected by Parkinson's deserves their own voice.

Why It Matters

You Were Never Meant to Figure Parkinson's Out Alone

The Parkinson's Club is a global community built for people living with Parkinson's and their care partners who want real support from others who truly understand the journey.

Members connect through topic-based clubs covering sleep, medication, movement, daily challenges, and more.

You can share experiences, learn practical strategies, and support each other without judgment.

Parkinson's journeys look different for everyone. Some people navigate it on their own. Others have a partner or loved one walking beside them. Both belong here.

And the best part?

The Club is completely free to join.

TrendSense

Understand Your Parkinson's Patterns with TrendSense

Track it. Understand it. Share it with your care team.

TrendSense helps you track and understand patterns in your Parkinson's.

Members can track medication timing, symptoms, sleep, meals, exercise, and on/off periods.

TrendSense analyzes these patterns so you can better understand how different factors affect your daily life.

Reports

Turn Your Tracking Into Real Insights

TrendSense generates reports that show patterns in symptoms, medication timing, sleep, and more.

Members will be able to generate clear, printable reports showing patterns in medication, symptoms, sleep, and more.

These reports can be:

  • shared with neurologists
  • printed for appointments
  • used to guide treatment discussions

Instead of trying to remember how the last few months felt, you'll have the information to show what's really been happening.

Why The Club Is Free

Why The Club Is Free

The goal of The Club is simple.

Make sure every person living with Parkinson's has access to support and community.

That's why membership to the club itself will always be free.

There may be optional deeper experiences like Inner Circle events in the future, but the core community will remain open to everyone living with Parkinson's and their care partners.

Founder Story

Why Bryce Built The Club

Bryce Perry was diagnosed with Parkinson's at age 40.

After years of sharing his journey online and connecting with thousands of others living with Parkinson's, one thing became clear.

People don't just want information.

They want connection with people who understand.

The Club exists to make that connection easier.

Honest Answer

So… what's the catch?

There isn't one.

The Club is a free community for people living with Parkinson's and their care partners.

You can connect with others, use the tools, and be part of the conversations without paying anything.

In the future there may be optional experiences like events or deeper Inner Circle programs for those who want them.

But the community itself will always remain open and free.

No tricks.

No hidden costs.

Just people helping each other navigate Parkinson's.
