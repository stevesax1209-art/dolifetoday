About the Show

Real Talk. Real Motion. Real Life.

Life in Motion is a Parkinson's podcast unlike any other - hosted by two people who actually live with the disease. Bryce Perry and Brian Campkin bring genuine lived experience to every conversation, mixing humor with honesty in a way that only people who truly understand can.

From navigating daily symptoms to staying active, finding community, and laughing when you have to - Life in Motion covers the real stuff that doesn't make it into doctor's appointments but matters deeply to the people living it.

If you're tired of podcasts that talk about Parkinson's from the outside, this is the show for you.

The Hosts

Meet Bryce & Brian

Both living with Parkinson's. Both determined to keep moving - and keep talking about it.

Bryce Perry

Co-Host · Founder, Doing Life Today

Diagnosed with Parkinson's at age 40, Bryce Perry turned a personal health crisis into a mission to help others navigate life with the disease.

He is the founder of Doing Life Today, a global Parkinson's community that reaches thousands of people each month through videos, podcasts, and educational resources.

Bryce is also the host of the podcast Living with Parkinson's: The Good, the Bad, and the Reality and co-host of Life in Motion – A Parkinson's Podcast, where real conversations about life with Parkinson's help people feel less alone and more empowered.

Through storytelling, humor, and practical insight, Bryce focuses on helping people move forward with confidence and continue Doing Life Today.

🎙️ Podcaster 🎤 Speaker 🏃 Advocate

Brian Campkin

Co-Host · Living with Parkinson's

Brian Campkin brings his lived experience with Parkinson's to every conversation on Life in Motion – A Parkinson's Podcast. Diagnosed in 2018, he shares practical insight, honesty, and encouragement with people navigating the realities of the disease.

Before becoming an advocate, Brian built a successful career in the information technology and telecommunications industry, where he developed a reputation for leadership and problem solving.

Brian is also a heart disease survivor. In 2007 he underwent triple bypass surgery after doctors discovered several blocked arteries, an experience that reshaped his outlook on life.

He is the author of From Survivor to Thriver – The Story of a Modern Day Tin Man, a memoir about resilience, purpose, and finding strength through adversity. Read the book →
