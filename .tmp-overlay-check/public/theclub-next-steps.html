One More Step

You’re Almost Done

Your waitlist signup went through. The next step is important: you must confirm your email before your place is fully secured.

Confirmation email sent to
1

Check your inbox now

Look for an email from office@dolifetoday.com asking you to confirm your place on The Club waitlist.

2

Check Spam, Junk, and Promotions

If you do not see an email from office@dolifetoday.com within a few minutes, check those folders next. This is the most common reason people miss the confirmation email.

3

Click the confirmation link

You must click the link in that email so your spot is confirmed and you can receive your invitation when your wave opens.

Important

If the email from office@dolifetoday.com lands in Spam or Junk, move it to your inbox before clicking the confirmation link. That gives future Club emails a much better chance of reaching you.
